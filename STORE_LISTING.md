EventHub is your all-in-one event platform for every occasion — from
weddings and funerals to corporate conferences and social gatherings.

FOR ATTENDEES
• Browse and discover events near you by category, region, and date
• RSVP instantly with free or paid ticket options
• Receive QR-coded tickets for seamless check-in
• Get real-time event updates and chat with other attendees
• Rate organizers and events after attending

FOR ORGANIZERS
• Create and manage events with a simple, guided form
• Track RSVPs, guest lists, and real-time check-ins with QR scanning
• Post live updates to keep attendees informed
• Boost your event to reach more people (featured listings)
• View analytics after your event ends
• Set up payout accounts to receive earnings

FOR ADMINS
• Review and approve events before they go live
• Manage users, suspend bad actors, and verify organizer badges
• Moderate reported content

All event data, bookings, and chats work offline so you never miss a beat,
even without an internet connection.

Download EventHub today and make every event unforgettable.
